ASBESTOS REMOVAL & DISPOSAL

Safe. Ethical. Officially Sanctioned.

Our Asbestos Services

Jali Ecosystems Limited is the destination of choice for Asbestos Containing Material (ACM) removal, transportation and disposal. Our services are safe, ethical and officially sanctioned, operating a NEMA licensed asbestos disposal landfill in Murang'a County.

  • NEMA licensed asbestos disposal facility in Murang'a County
  • Environmental and Social Impact Assessment (ESIA) process
  • Annual medical surveillance for all staff
  • Trained safety, health and environment (SHE) experts
  • Compliance with NEMA National Guidelines and OSHA 2007

Removal Process & Safety

Following NEMA National Guidelines and OSHA 2007, our trained personnel handle asbestos works with proper Personal Protective Equipment (PPE), medical surveillance, and governing legislations. Sheets are lowered using sturdy ropes to avoid breakages, and those showing signs of breakage are hand-water sprayed to contain fiber release. All works are documented and tracked for full compliance.

Key Clients & Projects

We've served diverse clientele including CFAO/KVM (Thika), Kevian Kenya Limited, Voi Town Lodge, Mantrac Kenya Limited (Nairobi and Mombasa), St. Patrick Catholic Church (Thika), Kileleshwa Covenant Community Church (Nairobi), and Wood Products (Industrial Area). Our NEMA-licensed disposal site has received material from these and many other industrial, commercial, and residential projects across Kenya.

AGROFORESTRY

Nature-Based Solutions

Integrated Agroforestry Systems

In partnership with Kenya Forestry Research Institute (KEFRI), we implement integrated agroforestry systems at Kibwezi. This initiative promotes ecological resilience, economic growth, and social empowerment for smallholder farmers using melia volkensii, nitrogen-fixing legumes, and pasture crops. Our models address soil fertility challenges and improve agricultural productivity.

Soil Fertility Improvement

Addressing soil fertility challenges through sustainable land use practices and green infrastructure development. Our agroforestry approach integrates melia volkensii plantations with food crops and pastures, providing long-term soil enrichment and carbon sequestration benefits for rural communities.

Sustainable Income Generation

Generating sustainable income for rural communities by promoting ecological resilience and climate change mitigation through community empowerment projects. Farmers receive training, planting materials, and ongoing support to establish and maintain agroforestry systems that provide timber, fuelwood, and food security while restoring degraded lands.
